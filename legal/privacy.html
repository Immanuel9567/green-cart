Privacy notice

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who we are

GreenCart, of the address on our contact page, Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. You can reach us at our contact page.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, delivery address, city and email address.
  • Payment information: handled entirely by our payment provider, Paystack. We never see or store your card number. We receive only a confirmation that a payment succeeded, plus a reference.
  • Technical information: whatever your browser and our host automatically record in order to serve the page.

What is stored in your browser

Your bag, saved items and the orders you place are stored in your own browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us and are not visible to us. Clearing your browser data removes them. If you change device, your order history does not follow you.

Why we use it

  • To take payment and deliver what you ordered.
  • To answer your questions and handle returns.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.
